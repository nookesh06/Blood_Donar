Blood Donor Management helps people find potential blood donors by blood group and location. Our goal is to make donor discovery clearer, faster, and easier during a time-sensitive need.

About the platform

Find donors

Search by blood group, city, or both to view matching donor records on the map.

Map-based discovery

View donor locations, estimate straight-line distance when your location is available, and request a route.

Manage your profile

Review and update your profile details from the profile page.

How it works

01 · Search

Choose a blood group, enter a location, or leave the fields empty to see all records returned by the system.

02 · Review

Check the donor details returned by the system. Verify availability and contact information before relying on a listing.

03 · Connect

Use the displayed contact details or map route to reach the donor, where appropriate.

Important: listings do not guarantee that a donor is currently available or medically eligible. Confirm details directly and follow guidance from a qualified healthcare provider.
